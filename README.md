# Module 13: Probability

## Overview

This repository contains my Probability Assignment, completed using Python simulations and statistical visualization. The assignment explores fundamental probability concepts and demonstrates how random experiments can be simulated and analyzed computationally.

## Objectives

- Understand experimental and theoretical probability.
- Estimate probabilities using repeated random simulations.
- Apply conditional probability and Bayes' theorem.
- Calculate statistical measures for a discrete random variable.
- Visualize an exponential distribution and its probability density function.
- Demonstrate the Central Limit Theorem using sample means.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Experimental Probability
- Simulate 10,000 coin tosses to estimate the probabilities of Heads and Tails.
- Simulate two dice and estimate the probability of obtaining a sum of 7.

### 2. Probability of at Least One Six
- Estimate the probability of obtaining at least one 6 in 10 rolls of a fair die using repeated trials.

### 3. Conditional Probability and Bayes' Theorem
- Estimate the probability of drawing a Red ball given that the previous draw was Blue.
- Use simulation to verify Bayes' theorem with consecutive draws and replacement.

### 4. Discrete Random Variable
- Generate 1,000 observations from a specified discrete probability distribution.
- Calculate the empirical mean, variance, and standard deviation.

### 5. Exponential Distribution
- Generate 2,000 observations from an exponential distribution with a mean of 5.
- Plot a density-normalized histogram and overlay the theoretical probability density function (PDF).

### 6. Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
- Generate a population of 10,000 random numbers from a uniform distribution.
- Draw 1,000 samples of size 30.
- Calculate the sample means and visualize their distribution.
